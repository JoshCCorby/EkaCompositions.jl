# Evidence audit and recommended framing

## Decision

The strongest supported direction is a **bounded empirical evaluation study**: how label policy, candidate-pool construction and the unit of recommendation change the interpretation of composition-recovery benchmarks. The software is the reproducibility vehicle. A preliminary paper is credible; a broad materials-discovery or new-method superiority paper is not currently supported. Publication value depends on a precise comparison to related work, not on making the model win.

## Dataset and experimental audit

**Population.** The retained snapshot has 24,020 records, collapsed to 12,506 canonical compositions: 5,359 positives and 7,147 unlabelled, with no unresolved groups or excluded records reported in the saved audit. Of the positives, 1,343 have mixed true/false provenance flags. H/halogen-bearing compositions remain eligible. Oxygen membership does not establish oxide chemistry. `snapshot.toml` records non-deprecated, three-element, O-containing query with `include_gnome=false`; this is recorded acquisition provenance, not an independent server-side GNoME exclusion audit.

**Endpoint.** At least one explicit `theoretical=false` record makes the original composition group positive. All true is unlabelled. This is an experimental-provenance proxy. Alternative policies either remove mixed groups or move them into the unlabelled pool; neither policy has been independently validated as chemical truth. Database recording and research effort can drive both labels and training frequencies. No SCAR (selected completely at random) labeling assumption has been justified. The measured endpoint remains withheld observed labels, so no synthesis-probability calibration is claimed.

**Units and splits.** Formula holdout removes floor(P/5) positives and includes *all* eligible unlabelled compositions. System holdout chooses floor(S/5) systems from a common original universe and includes only their policy-eligible candidates, training on positives from other systems. This changes the task distribution. Formula grouping prevents proportional-formula/polymorph splitting; system disjointness prevents exact element-set overlap but permits related chemical systems and oxygen-dominated cosine overlap. Same integer seed in different hash domains does not make the two designs paired samples.

**Budgets and seeds.** K=100 primary, K=20/50/200 secondary; 20 hash holdouts (0–19); random ranking seed 10000+s; tie seed 20260901; model initialization seed 20260902. Models are deterministic conditional on these inputs; 120 fits are 2 designs × 3 policies × 20 splits, not 120 independent datasets. Alternative policies share training in each design/seed and yield identical factors. This further limits an independence interpretation.

**Metrics.** Hits@K, Hits/K, Hits/h, enrichment=(Hits/K)/(h/N), and expected uniform-random hits=Kh/N. All denominators refer to actual branch/split pools. A mean enrichment must average per-split enrichments, not divide aggregate mean hits by aggregate mean expectation when prevalence varies. K=100 is a row budget, not 100 independent systems, laboratory trials or discoveries. Do not call Hits/K measured precision over negatives.

**Uncertainty.** Existing outputs provide paired within-branch split differences, ranges, medians and signs. They do not support population CIs from split SEMs, seed-level t-tests, bootstrap of selected rows, or claims of statistical significance. A future population inference would need a justified sampling target and dependence-aware analysis. Descriptive sensitivity is adequate for the proposed paper.

**Baselines and gaps.** Uniform random expectation and realization, training-element popularity, max count-vector cosine, fixed factor model. No published state-of-the-art model is fairly benchmarked on these same splits. Seko's released scores lack demonstrated training independence and have different coverage by label. A direct pair-frequency control exists only in the dirty tree. No weak-zero ablation, initialization/rank sweep, prospective test, independent data source or matched cross-design pool study was found.

**Cost.** Historical documentation reports total pair fitting 18.71 s and ranking 0.83 s for the real primary run, excluding startup, validation, I/O, analysis and reproduction. These are local phase timings, not end-to-end time, peak memory, or portable speed claims. Independent Python verification during this audit is separately timed/recorded. No costly experiment or full refit was launched.

The original formula-policy similarity comparison is weak (+0.5 mean hits), and at K=20 it is −1.85 versus popularity; at K=200 it is −0.35. The pair model also changes sign versus popularity at K=200 under the original composition policy (+1.95). Therefore “model A is strongest under design B” is too broad without specifying budget and policy. Table and figures must retain all cutoffs.

The tracked findings' statement that the similarity reversal “identifies ... the mechanism” is too causal for this design. Use: **“The reversal is consistent with dependence on shared-system proximity, but the two protocols also differ in their candidate pools and prevalence.”** No application/documentation fix is made here; this is manuscript guidance.

## Three candidate framings

| Framing | Research question / intended reader | Contribution / evidence available | Missing evidence / main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Empirical evaluation case study — recommended** | How do provenance policies and holdout design alter fixed-budget recovery and its interpretation? Materials-informatics evaluators. | Six branches, four methods, deterministic paired comparisons, later concentration and compute-stability diagnostics. | All-method system-aware comparison, concise closest-work assessment, explicit data availability. Risk: useful but incremental single-snapshot case study. |
| B. Reproducible research-software paper | Can composition-level PU experiments be implemented with inspectable provenance and label isolation? Research-software users/maintainers. | Tested Julia interfaces, offline examples, frozen protocol identities, independent analyzers, local full-rerun evidence. | Independent user/adoption evidence and comparison with existing tools; stronger public real example. Risk: engineering breadth without a distinct research need beyond existing tooling. |
| C. Element-pair methods paper | Does low-rank pair completion add reliable unseen-system recovery over frequencies? Recommender/materials ML readers. | Explicit objective, numerical tests, fixed-compute comparisons and cap sensitivity. | Pair-frequency result, weak-zero ablation, system-level benefit, justified optimization choice and wider transfer evaluation. Risk: standard model family, ties, weak/conditional effects and no validated synthesis endpoint. |

A is the minimum credible direction today. It does not need a new neural model or a favorable additional experiment. If a close literature comparison leaves insufficient distinctive insight, narrow further to a transparent technical report/software note; do not manufacture novelty by expanding the title.

Primary literature and access limits are recorded in [07-literature.md](07-literature.md). Novelty remains provisional: existing work already covers composition recommendation, PU synthesizability, dataset bias and misleading OOD partitions. Eka's possible contribution is the specific controlled combination of these diagnostics and its reproducible evidence trail.
