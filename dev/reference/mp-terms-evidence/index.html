Materials Project terms: evidence and handling

Reviewed 31 August 2026. The project owner supplied a copy of the Materials Project Terms of Use in response to the outstanding terms request. Intended source: MP Terms of Use. The exact supplied text is preserved locally; SHA-256: e614dcdf306ef7e324136933fc99361a8b2c702c93736340c5ddf4df8dad6c3e.

On 31 August 2026 the official page was also retrieved interactively at the intended URL, which redirected to https://next-gen.materialsproject.org/about/terms. The visible page identified website revision 9f1b7077 and database version v2026.04.13. It confirmed the CC BY 4.0 Content grant, the credit/licence/change-notice requirements, the separate CC BY-NC 4.0 terms for GNoME, and the request to contact MP for guidance before repackaging and publicly redistributing MP data. No form was submitted, message sent or new API query made during this read-only check.

What the text establishes

The supplied terms expressly permit collecting database content through the API for analysis and presenting processed results with proper attribution. They state that downloaded Content is covered by Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, including copying, distribution, transmission and adaptation without specific permission from MP when proper attribution is given.

CC BY 4.0 requires appropriate credit, the licence link, retention of supplied notices and an indication of modifications; do not imply endorsement or impose incompatible restrictions. It permits commercial use. This data licence is separate from Eka's MIT licence. The terms also prohibit direct website scraping and request advance contact for large downloads. This project uses the API; this review makes no new download and sends no message to MP.

Application to this project

Use the supplied terms as the working basis for preparing attributed research results and covered MP data. A separate provider permission request is not a blanket prerequisite for uses already permitted by this grant. Do not label the grant absent simply because our web reader could not retrieve the page.

The snapshot's retained MP fields are material_id, composition, formula_pretty, theoretical, database_IDs, and deprecated. Derived artifacts include canonical composition groups, provenance labels, splits, rankings, aggregate metrics and plots. The general Content grant supports their preparation with appropriate attribution insofar as they are covered content; this is not independent field-by-field provider confirmation.

Preserve MP citation, database version, retrieval date, source route and changes (normalization, grouping, labels, splitting and scoring). Keep GNoME excluded. MP identifiers do not grant rights to separately obtain or redistribute the underlying ICSD/Pauling/other source records. No such original records or crystal structures were requested by the exporter. Retain any specific exception or source notice encountered during final artifact review.

Evidence limits and release status

The owner-supplied copy does not include its browser URL or a licence-version date, but the independent read-only page check above confirms that its material licensing provisions match the official page visible on the review date. The page does not display an effective date for the Content grant. Its 10 October 2011 effective date belongs to the privacy-policy section and must not be represented as the date of the CC BY 4.0 grant. This remains a project evidence review, not an institution's legal opinion.

The source-only Git archive has a separate release-readiness review. Review any proposed data bundle for exceptions, credentials, provenance and attribution before publication; do not automatically add datasets to Git. Seek clarification only for a concrete unresolved exception or repackaging question. No data release or external message is made by this review.
